## Why Choose a Private Tour?

Start with the fact that reframes the whole decision: with Adventure Tours Vernal, there is no crowded, mixed-in-with-strangers alternative to escape from. Every tour is already yours alone. So choosing a "private tour" isn't buying your way out of a bad experience — it's dialing the *good* one up to its most personal setting. The smaller the party you bring, the more completely the day bends to it.

That personalization shows up first in **pacing**. On any tour with more machines, the tempo naturally settles to an average — comfortable for everyone, tailored to no one. Ride as a couple or a small family and that compromise disappears. Want to linger at Moonshine Arch until the shadows are right, then open it up a little on the sandy stretches of Doc's Beach? Prefer to take the whole three hours slow, stopping often for the history and the quiet? Either way, the pace is a conversation between your group and your guide, not a setting the group size decides for you.

The same is true of **flexibility**. A small private tour can change its mind. If the light is better for photos on the way back than on the way out, you flip the plan. If your group is captivated by a rock-art panel and wants ten more minutes, you take them. If someone would rather trade the wheel and just take in the scenery, you swap on the spot. None of that requires negotiating with a dozen other people — it's just your party and Dave and Trudy, adjusting as the day unfolds.

For **couples and anniversaries**, that intimacy is the entire appeal. A shared Kawasaki KRX 1000, a trail most visitors never see, and a guide who gives you room rather than a schedule — it's an unusually personal way to mark an occasion.
