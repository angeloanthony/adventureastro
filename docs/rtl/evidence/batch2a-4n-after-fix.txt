Each of these renders a reversed bracket, or moves the run it encloses, because the
neutral resolves to the paragraph direction rather than to its content (UAX #9 N2).
Fix by asking `src/lib/bidi.ts` for the run — phoneDisplay, currencyDisplay, brandRun —
never by inserting <bdi> or a control character at the call site, which is the
convention this gate exists to keep enforceable.
