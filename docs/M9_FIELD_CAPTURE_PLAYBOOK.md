## 0. Standing field rules (read once, apply every day)

- **Legal/ethics (non-negotiable).** DNM is NPS land — **no drone launch/landing/operation**. **High Uintas Wilderness — no drones.** Flaming Gorge NRA / Ashley NF non-Wilderness and Utah State Parks — **drone only where the managing agency's current rule permits; carry written confirmation.** Never fly without it.
- **Rock art & trackways:** never chalk, wet, touch, cast, or move anything. Shoot in low raking light instead. **Never publish precise coordinates** of remote panels (site protection; M6 protection rule). Keep captions/alt deliberately general on sensitive sites.
- **Every keeper needs a field note.** At capture, log a one-line dated, generally-located observation in Dave/Trudy's voice (feeds the EEAT caption layer, §5). Example: *"On the water by 7 before the north wind builds — Dave, Aug 2026."*
- **Shoot for the crop.** Frame every hero candidate so the subject survives a **1200×630** (1.91:1) og:image crop *and* a 4:3/16:9 body crop. Leave headroom.
- **Redundancy:** two cards, back up nightly, RAW + in-camera JPEG. Bracket high-contrast scenes (canyon shade + sky).

### FD1 — Downtown Vernal & the UTV Trails *(the commercial core; shoulder-season light, any weather works)*

- **Staging:** 1935 S 1500 E (tour departure). Book a real tour slot to shoot live.
- **Estimated order (one full day):**
  1. **Dawn/early AM (garage + staging):** fleet line-up, KRX cockpit + FOX shock detail, Dave's pre-ride safety briefing, child-in-cage buckle. *(controlled light, gets the evergreen commercial detail before the ride)*
  2. **Mid-morning ride out:** chest-mount POV b-roll across the trail; Dave & Trudy each leading on their own KRX; guests dusty and happy.
  3. **Golden hour:** **Asphalt Ridge fleet on the ridgeline** (the missing 5th-trail hero) + tour highlight-reel closers.
  4. **Late/town:** Main Street dino sculptures + pink "Dinah," Utah Field House Dinosaur Garden, restaurant storefronts + one dish each.
- **Expected deliverables:** ≥8 P1 stills (fleet, ridge, cockpit, child-in-cage, briefing, Dave+Trudy guiding), the **tour highlight reel** (60–90 s), 4+ verticals, 1 long-form "5 Trails" b-roll bin.
- **Backup options:** rain/dust-storm → shift entirely to garage/cockpit/briefing + indoor Field House + Main Street under cloud (all weather-proof); if a tour cancels, stage a fleet run with Dave/Trudy only.
- **Weather:** wind kicks dust (good for atmosphere, bad for lenses — carry covers); midday flat light → save Asphalt Ridge for golden hour.
- **Drone:** BLM/trail land + FAA rules — **verify per staging area**; if cleared, 8-machine convoy in a canyon + Asphalt Ridge reveal. Otherwise ground-only.

### FD2 — Dinosaur National Monument *(shoulder-season mornings; ~20 min E to the Utah side)*

- **Estimated order:** Quarry Exhibit Hall at opening (Wall of Bones + touch-the-fossil) → Fossil Discovery Trail bone in situ → Cub Creek auto-tour (lizard panel in low raking light, Josie Morris cabin ext+int, Split Mountain cut) → McConkie Ranch panel on the drive back (Dry Fork).

- **Weather:** midday sun flattens petroglyphs — shoot panels early/late; summer thunderstorms build afternoons (leave exposed Cub Creek overlooks when they threaten).
- **Drone:** **NONE (NPS land — prohibited).** Get Split Mountain scale from overlooks + long lens.

### FD3 — Red Fleet + Steinaker State Parks *(calm dawn; ~15 min / ~5–10 min N)*

### FD4 — Ashley National Forest & High Uintas *(SUMMER-ONLY — shoot in Aug, before the window closes; V snow/road status)*

### FD5 — Flaming Gorge *(spring–fall, full day; ~1 hr N)*

- **Drone:** Flaming Gorge NRA / Ashley NF rules — **verify**; if cleared, Red Canyon rim reveal + dam orbit (never over Wilderness).

### FD6 — Scenic Drives *(FALL for color; ~V seasonal closures)*

- **Backup:** early snow closes the upper loop → shoot the lower byway + Sheep Creek (lower elevation) and reschedule Red Cloud; overcast → the "Drive Through the Ages" signs shoot fine flat.
- **Weather:** **peak aspen is a ~2-week window (late Sep–mid Oct, elevation-dependent)** — watch the color report and go when it turns; upper roads close with first heavy snow.
- **Drone:** Ashley NF non-Wilderness only, verified — Sheep Creek fold from above; Red Cloud aspen stands.

## 5. Publication Workflow (per captured asset)

Respects the **frozen image architecture**: heroes are metadata-only in `public/`; rendered body images live in `src/` and go through `astro:assets`. This playbook *stages* the workflow; integration happens in the editorial window (no page edit here).

**1. Filename convention (adopt for all NEW assets — do not inherit the legacy spaced names).**
Lowercase kebab-case, cluster-prefixed, descriptive, optional light/time tag:
`{cluster}-{subject}-{qualifier}.webp`
- `utv-asphalt-ridge-fleet-goldenhour.webp`, `utv-krx-cockpit-fox-shock.webp`, `utv-child-in-cage.webp`
- `dnm-quarry-wall-of-bones.webp`, `dnm-cubcreek-lizard-panel.webp`
- `redfleet-trackway-lowlight.webp`, `redfleet-paddle-to-tracks-dawn.webp`
- `uintas-alpine-lake-reflection-dawn.webp`, `gorge-red-canyon-overlook-goldenhour.webp`
- `scenic-redcloud-aspen-gold.webp`
Video: same scheme, `.mp4`/`.mov`; verticals suffixed `-vertical`; thumbnails `-thumb`.

**2. Alt-text approach (page-specific, honest to the frame).**
Pattern: *"[subject] at [place near Vernal], [what's happening / relationship shown]."* No unverified numbers. **Rock-art alt stays deliberately non-specific** (no coordinates/site names). Heroes are metadata-only → supply **`heroAlt`** in frontmatter; body images supply **`<img alt>`**. Write both; never reuse the caption verbatim as alt.
- Ex (hero): *"Kawasaki KRX side-by-sides lined up on Asphalt Ridge above Vernal at golden hour, guides ready to lead a backcountry tour."*
- Ex (body, rock art): *"A Fremont lizard petroglyph on a desert-varnished panel in Dinosaur National Monument, lit by low raking light."*

**3. Metadata.**
Embed IPTC caption/creator ("Adventure Tours Vernal — Dave/Trudy Wilson"), capture date, and a general location (no precise rock-art GPS — strip/round EXIF coordinates on sensitive sites). The dated **field note** captured in the field becomes the on-page **caption** (EEAT proof), not the alt.

**4. Optimization workflow.**
- Cull/edit RAW → export master.
- **Heroes:** export **WebP/AVIF, long edge ≥1600 px but file ≤500 KB** to clear the existing zod hero gate (validator: heroImage must be `.webp/.avif` and ≤500 KB).
